# The INW Discovery Zone

Everything in this book keeps going online in the **INW Discovery Zone** — four collections where you can test every idea for yourself with interactive simulations, calculators, and activities:

- **Nature's Calc Collection** — the φ (golden conjugate *x*) labs and number-pattern labs: Phi Division, Phi Spiral, Phi Derivation, Phi Construction, Finding Phi, DIY Caliper & Ruler, Golden Angle Plant Lab, Parastichy Explorer, Spiral Zoetrope, Phyllotaxis, Pascal / Meru Prastāra, Pingala, Mod-n, and 3D Maha Meru.

- **Pendulum Lab Collection** — one progressive oscillation lab with Basic, Pendulum Wave, Blackburn, Coupled, Wilberforce, and Newton's Cradle setups for period, energy, resonance, coupled modes, and momentum transfer through collisions.

- **Waves Lab Collection** — the Mechanical Waves and Standing Waves sims for amplitude, frequency, wavelength, speed, superposition, and resonance.

- **Thinking Lab Collection** — the Wheelwork Map (synthesis), The Long View (deep time), and the Question Wall (inquiry).

**Search across all four at once.** The Discovery Zone has one search box at the top — type a word like *wave*, *resonance*, *pendulum*, *spiral*, or *phi* and it instantly gathers every matching simulation, calculator, and activity from all four collections in one place.

Scan the code or visit **imaginenatureswheelwork.com/discoveryzone.html**
