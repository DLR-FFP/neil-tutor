$$Rs = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{2}{10^2}\right)^n = 1 + \frac{2}{100} + \left(\frac{2}{100}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{2}{100}\right)^3 + \cdots$$
